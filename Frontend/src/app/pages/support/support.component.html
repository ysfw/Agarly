Quick Help

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I list an item?

Click the "Add Item" button, upload photos, fill in the details about your item, and submit. Your item will be visible to neighbors in your area.

How does verification work?

Verification requires confirming your email, phone number, and address. Verified members get a badge and are trusted more in the community.

What if an item is damaged?

Contact support immediately. We recommend taking photos and documenting the condition. Security deposits help protect against damages.

How do I report a user?

Go to the user's profile and click "Report User". Provide details about the issue and our team will investigate.
